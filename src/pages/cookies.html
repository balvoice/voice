Cookie policy

This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies this website uses, why, and how you can control them.

Policy version 2026-10 · Last updated: [date of publication]

Your choices

When you first visit, we ask whether you agree to optional cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are always used because the site cannot work without them. Everything else stays off unless you switch it on.

You can change your mind at any time:

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal of analytics.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in a comparable way. In this policy, "cookies" covers both.

Analytics

We do not currently use any analytics cookies. If we add an analytics service in future, it will only run if you switch on Analytics, and we will update this table and ask for your consent again.

Third-party services

Sign-in. Accounts are managed by Supabase, our authentication and database provider. If you choose Continue with Google, you are sent to Google to sign in, and Google's own cookies and privacy policy apply on its pages. If bot protection is enabled on sign-in forms, Cloudflare Turnstile may run a short check in your browser; it is used only for security.

This website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests the fonts, Google receives your IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the site may not work, and we will ask for your cookie choices on every visit.
