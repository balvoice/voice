A familiar voice carries further

Announcements from the pulpit reach the people in the room. Printed flyers and text messages are easy to overlook. A phone call carrying a recorded message from your pastor or ministry leader is heard in their own words, with their own warmth.

With a voice broadcast, that message reaches every registered member and contact on your list at once — without volunteers spending days on the phone.

From recording to report

Record → Upload → Schedule → Deliver → Report. Here is what happens at each stage.

  1. Record

    Your pastor or leader records the message, or we help produce it. We review it for clarity and length.

  2. Upload

    Your contact list is uploaded. Numbers are cleaned, duplicates removed, and opted-out contacts excluded.

  3. Schedule

    Pick the day and time — before a crusade, ahead of Sunday, or the evening before an event.

  4. Deliver

    The system calls each number automatically and plays the message when the call is answered.

  5. Report

    You receive a delivery report showing who was reached and who could not be.

Messaging guidelines

Good practice that protects your recipients and your ministry's reputation.

  • Call only people who agreed to be contacted. Members, registered visitors, and people who signed up at an event. Lists should come from your own records, not purchased databases.
  • Identify yourself in the first few seconds. Say the name of your church or ministry and who is speaking.
  • Include an opt-out. Every message tells listeners how to stop receiving calls.
  • Respect calling hours. Calls are scheduled within a reasonable daytime and early-evening window.

Tip: State the date, time, and place of an event twice in your recording. Listeners often miss details the first time.

Questions

How long should the message be?

Between 30 and 60 seconds works well for most announcements. Shorter messages are more likely to be heard in full, and message length is one of the main factors in campaign cost.

What format should our contact list be in?

A spreadsheet (.xlsx) or CSV file with one phone number per row. A name column is optional. We clean the list and remove duplicates before the campaign starts.

Can we record the message in a local language?

Yes. The system plays whatever audio you provide, so your message can be in any language your congregation understands.

What happens if someone doesn't answer?

If you enable retries, the number is called again after a set interval. Calls that are still not answered are marked "no answer" in your report.

How much does a campaign cost?

Cost depends on the number of contacts, the length of the message, retries, and production needs. See campaign cost factors to build an estimate.
