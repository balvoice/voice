About BalVoice

We help organizations deliver important messages to the people who need to hear them — reliably, responsibly, and at scale.

Our story

BalVoice was set up to solve a practical problem: organizations with something important to say often have no reliable way to say it to everyone at once. Calling members one by one takes days. Text messages are easily missed. A recorded voice message, delivered automatically, closes that gap.

Mission

To give churches, organizations, and campaigns a dependable way to reach thousands of people through one voice — with the scheduling, tracking, and reporting needed to know their message was delivered.

What we stand for

Reliability

When a message has to go out at a set time, it goes out. We plan campaigns carefully and monitor them until the last call is made.

Consent and respect

We deliver only to people who agreed to be contacted, give every recipient a way to opt out, and call within reasonable hours.

Transparency

Clear cost factors before a campaign, and a full delivery report after it. You always know what you are paying for and what you received.

Neutrality

We provide the same service, on the same terms, to every lawful organization — regardless of faith, party, or cause.
