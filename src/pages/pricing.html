Campaign cost factors

Every campaign is priced on what it actually uses. There are no fixed packages — the cost follows from the size of your list, the length of your message, and how many attempts are made.

How cost is calculated

Airtime is the largest part of most campaigns. It is charged per minute of call time, so the formula multiplies everything that adds call time.

Total Cost =
  (Contacts × Average Minutes × Airtime Rate × Attempts)
  + Platform Fee
  + Message Production

Airtime rates vary by network and destination, and change over time. We quote the current rate for your campaign before it is scheduled.

The six factors

Understanding these helps you plan a campaign that fits your budget.

  1. Contact list size

    The number of phone numbers to be called. Cleaning the list first — removing duplicates and invalid numbers — avoids paying for calls that cannot connect.

  2. Message duration

    Longer messages use more airtime per call. Depending on the network, calls may be billed per second or rounded up to the next full minute, so a 62-second message can cost the same as a 2-minute one.

  3. Retry attempts

    Retries call busy or unanswered numbers again. They increase the number of people reached, and the number of call attempts.

  4. Answer rate

    The share of calls that are picked up. It depends on your audience, the time of day, and whether recipients recognise the caller. A higher answer rate means fewer retries are needed.

  5. Platform fees

    Covers campaign setup, list processing, scheduling, dialing infrastructure, and reporting.

  6. Production costs

    Optional. Scriptwriting, professional voice recording, translation, or audio editing if you do not supply a finished recording.

Ways to keep costs down

  • Keep the message short. Say what matters in 30 to 45 seconds. If billing rounds up per minute, staying under 60 seconds matters most.
  • Clean your list. Remove duplicates, wrong numbers, and people who have opted out before you upload.
  • Choose the calling time carefully. Calling when your audience is free improves the answer rate and reduces retries.
  • Match retries to your goal. One or two retries usually balance reach and cost. More retries reach more people at a higher cost per contact.
