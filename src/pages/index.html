How a campaign runs

Five steps from recording to report. Most of the work happens on our side once your message and list are ready.

  1. Record

    Your message is recorded as a clear audio file, usually 30 to 60 seconds.

  2. Upload

    The audio and your contact list are uploaded and checked for errors and duplicates.

  3. Schedule

    You choose the date, time, calling window, and how many retries to allow.

  4. Deliver

    The system dials each number automatically and plays your message when answered.

  5. Report

    Results are collected for every call and compiled into a campaign report.

Know what happened to every call

Each attempt is tracked from dial to hang-up. When the campaign ends, results are compiled into a report you can review and download.

  • Results per phone number, including retries
  • Totals by outcome for the whole campaign
  • Downloadable report for your own records
Call outcomes recorded in campaign reports
OutcomeWhat it means
AnsweredThe call connected and the message was played.
BusyThe line was engaged. The number is queued for a retry if retries are enabled.
No answerThe phone rang but was not picked up. Retried if enabled.
UnreachableThe phone was switched off, out of coverage, or the number is invalid.

Responsible by design

Reaching people at scale comes with a duty to respect them. These rules apply to every campaign we run.

Consent first

We only deliver to people who have agreed to receive calls from your organization. You confirm this before every campaign.

A way to opt out

Every message explains how to stop receiving calls. Opted-out numbers are removed from all future campaigns.

Respectful hours

Calls are placed only within a set daily calling window, so messages arrive at reasonable times.
