A neutral communication service

BalVoice provides the infrastructure for delivering recorded messages. We serve candidates, parties, and civic groups across the political spectrum on the same terms, and we do not endorse or promote any party, candidate, or position.

Every campaign is subject to the same requirements: messages go only to people who have agreed to be contacted, every message identifies who it is from, and every recipient can opt out.

What campaigns use it for

Voice broadcast is suited to messages that are time-sensitive, need to reach many people, and benefit from a personal voice.

Campaign announcements
Share manifesto launches, policy positions, or campaign news directly with supporters in the candidate's own voice.
Voter communication
Provide factual information to registered contacts — such as polling dates and times, and how to verify registration.
Community outreach
Reach residents of a constituency or ward with updates on local projects, consultations, and meetings.
Event reminders
Remind supporters about rallies, town halls, door-to-door days, and volunteer briefings the day before or the morning of.
Mobilization messaging
Coordinate volunteers and party agents with timely instructions and reminders during busy campaign periods.
Contact groups
Organise supporters and volunteers by constituency, ward, or role, and send each group the message that applies to them.
Delivery reporting
See which contacts were reached. Those who were not can be saved as a group so field teams can follow up.
Recurring reminders
Schedule weekly or monthly updates to the same groups, with your approved caller ID shown on every call.

How it works

The same five-stage process used for every campaign, with a compliance review before scheduling.

  1. Record

    The message is recorded and reviewed for sender identification and an opt-out statement.

  2. Upload

    Your consented contact list is uploaded, cleaned, and checked against the opt-out register.

  3. Schedule

    Choose the start time, calling window, and retries. Scheduling respects permitted calling hours.

  4. Deliver

    Calls are placed automatically and the message plays when answered.

  5. Report

    Outcomes for every number are compiled into a report for your team.

Responsible communication guidelines

These conditions apply to all political and civic campaigns. We may decline or pause a campaign that does not meet them.

  • Consent-based lists only. Contacts must have agreed to receive communication from your campaign or organization — for example by joining as a member, volunteer, or supporter. You confirm consent before each campaign.
  • Clear identification. Each message states who it is from and on whose behalf it is sent.
  • Opt-out in every message. Recipients are told how to stop future calls. Opt-out requests are honoured across all future campaigns.
  • Truthful content. Messages must not contain false information about voting procedures, dates, or eligibility, and must not impersonate another person or organization.
  • No harassment or incitement. Content that threatens, intimidates, or incites violence or hatred will not be delivered.
  • Respectful calling hours. Calls are placed only within a set daily window and in line with any applicable electoral or telecommunications rules.
  • Legal compliance. You are responsible for meeting the electoral and data protection laws that apply to your campaign. We will support you with records of consent and opt-outs.

Note: Rules for political calling vary by country and may change during election periods. Please check current requirements with the relevant electoral and communications authorities before your campaign.

Questions

Do you work with all parties?

Yes. The service is available to any lawful party, candidate, or civic organization on the same terms and under the same guidelines.

Can you provide a list of voters to call?

No. Campaigns must use contacts who have agreed to hear from them. We help you clean and manage your own list, but we do not sell or supply contact data.

Will you review our message before it is sent?

Yes. Each recording is checked for sender identification, an opt-out statement, and compliance with the guidelines above before it is scheduled.

How quickly can a campaign go out?

Once the recording and list are approved, a campaign can be scheduled for the time you choose. Delivery time depends on list size and the calling window.
