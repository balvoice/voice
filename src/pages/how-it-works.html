How the technology works

A voice broadcast moves through five parts of a system — from the platform where your message is stored to the report that tells you what happened to every call.

System overview

  1. Voice PlatformStores audio, contacts, and schedules
  2. Dialer EnginePlaces calls and plays the message
  3. Telephone NetworkCarries calls to mobile and fixed lines
  4. Recipient PhoneRings, answers, hears the message
  5. Reporting SystemCollects and compiles call results

Each stage in detail

What each component does, and what it means for your campaign.

  1. Voice Platform

    Audio storage · Contact management · Scheduling

    The platform is where a campaign is put together. Your recorded message is stored as an audio file, your contact list is imported and cleaned, and the campaign schedule is saved.

    • Audio is stored securely and checked for format and length
    • Numbers are standardised, duplicates removed, and opted-out contacts excluded
    • The schedule records start time, daily calling window, and retry rules
    • Contacts are kept in reusable groups, so a campaign can go to one or several groups without uploading a list again
    • Messages can be an uploaded recording or typed text converted to speech in English, Twi, Hausa, or Ewe, personalised with each contact's name

  2. Dialer Engine

    Automated dialing

    At the scheduled time, the dialer begins working through the contact list. It places many calls in parallel, detects when a call is answered, and plays your message.

    • Calls are placed only inside the calling window you set
    • Busy and unanswered numbers are queued for retry after the interval you choose
    • The campaign can be paused or stopped at any point
    • Calls display your approved caller ID — a brand name or your own number
    • Campaigns can repeat daily, weekly, or monthly, calling each group as it stands on the day

  3. Telephone Network

    Call routing

    Calls travel over standard telephone networks to mobile and landline numbers. The network returns a signal for each attempt — whether the call connected, the line was busy, the phone rang without an answer, or the number could not be reached.

  4. Recipient Phone

    Message delivery

    The recipient's phone rings like any normal call. When they answer, your recorded message plays from the start. No app, internet connection, or smartphone is needed — any phone that can receive calls will work.

  5. Reporting System

    Call tracking

    The result of every attempt is recorded as it happens. Call results such as answered, busy, and unreachable are collected into reports, giving you totals for the campaign and a line-by-line record for each number.

    • Outcome, attempt count, and time of each call
    • Campaign totals by outcome
    • Downloadable report for your records and follow-up
    • Contacts who were not reached can be saved as a new group for a follow-up campaign
    • Call results can be sent to your own systems through a webhook

Call outcomes

Every attempt ends in one of these results. They appear in your delivery report.

Data, consent, and security

Your contact lists and recordings belong to you. They are handled with that in mind.

Consent records
Before each campaign, you confirm that recipients agreed to be contacted. This confirmation is stored with the campaign.
Opt-out register
Opt-out requests are added to a register that is checked before every campaign, so those numbers are never called again.
Restricted access
Campaign data is visible only to authorised users on your account.
Secure uploads
Uploaded files are checked for type and size and transferred over encrypted connections.
