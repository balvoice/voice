Who we are

BalVoice [registered company name, registration number, address] is responsible for personal data collected through this website. Contact: [privacy contact email].

Website visitors

  • Campaign requests: when you use the request form, we collect your name, organization, email, phone number, and campaign details, and use them only to respond to you and prepare a quote.
  • Cookies: see our cookie policy. Optional cookies are only used with your consent.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider may record IP addresses and request details for security. [retention period]

Clients and account holders

When you create an account, we process your name, organization (if given), and either your email address and a securely hashed password, your Google account email and name (if you sign in with Google), or your mobile number (if you sign in by phone). We use this to provide the service, keep your account secure, and contact you about it.

  • Sign-in codes by SMS are sent through our SMS provider, BMS Africa. Only your phone number and the code are passed to them. We keep a hashed record of when codes were sent, to limit abuse.
  • Authentication and data storage are provided by Supabase. [hosting region]
  • Security logs: sign-in attempts, IP addresses, and device information may be recorded to detect and prevent fraud. [retention period]

Call recipients

Contact lists are supplied by our clients. For this data the client decides who is called and why, and we process it on their instructions. Clients must confirm that every recipient has agreed to receive calls.

  • Data we hold: phone numbers, any names or fields the client supplies for personalization, and call results (answered, busy, no answer, unreachable, time, attempts).
  • Opt-out: anyone can ask not to be called again. Opted-out numbers are kept on a register so they are excluded from future campaigns. See how to stop receiving calls.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the service — such as telephone network operators to connect calls, and our hosting provider — and when required by law. We do not sell personal data. [list of key providers and locations]

Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections, access is limited to authorized staff and client users, and uploaded files are checked before processing.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or object to its use. Contact [privacy contact email]. If you were called as part of a client's campaign, we will pass your request to that organization and remove your number from future calls.
