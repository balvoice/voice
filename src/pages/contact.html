Stop receiving calls

If you received a call delivered through BalVoice and no longer wish to receive them, we will remove your number.

  • During a call: follow the opt-out instruction given in the message.
  • Online: enter the number you were called on below. It is excluded from every campaign we deliver, for all organizations.
  • By contacting us: send the phone number you were called on to [opt-out email or number].
